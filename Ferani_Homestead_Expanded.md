Home on the range!

A bunch of new functions to items from the Homestead DLC! I have tried to keep things as balanced as possible, while also trying to keep the new functions fun and usable.

Mod includes:

Honey pots can store honey, honeycomb and other honey products
Vintage jars can store jarred foods and jam
Cheese wheels can store cheese and prevent it from spoiling
The knitting basket can hold fabrics
Hanging meats store meats and prevent them from spoiling
Butter churns can use cream or milk to make butter, and butters are cheaper to make using churns. Butter doesn't spoil while stored in the churn
The spinning wheel helps to process fabrics cheaper and with new recipes (and spins while crafting!)
The sewing machine is now Tier 4
The kitchen bench is now Tier 4
The Iron Stove and Homestead Stove share recipes
Homestead items are available for crafting in more different types of benches
The weather vane functions as a lightning rod
The well stores water and produces a small amount of water to your water network, filling itself up over time. Place containers inside to fill them up
The pitchfork is upgraded (and more expensive)
Fiber can be crafted into fiber bales for storage, just like the wood and stone piles
The silo holds more types of animal feed, has a lot more slots and prevents spoiling
The red refrigerator prevents spoiling

Installation:

WITH EXMODZ in the ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP)
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" (double-click on the mod name)
Merge it with other mods / by itself
Play!

WITH PAK in the ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (NOT RECOMMENDED, SINCE THE MOD MAY EXPIRE FROM WEEK TO WEEK):
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Extract Mod from PAK File"
Choose the PAK-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP)
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" (double-click on the mod name)
Merge it with other mods / by itself
Play!
