All animals have the grazing ability, so they can find their own food. Part of the Obedience Training Collection.

You're not the one paying the bills around here, find your own food!

The Grazing Ability is quite far up in the talent tree, and some tames and mounts lack the ability entirely. Now all animals have the ability by default, so they can graze on wild fruits and vegetables or animal carcasses - or turn it off if you want to control their feeding. Lock up your garden if you don't want a hungry beastie to steal your crops!

Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_GrantedAuras
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats

If you have any other mods that affect the Grazing Ability, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):

Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
