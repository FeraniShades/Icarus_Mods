If you can't tame a Moa in the wild, store-bought is fine.

It's no shame to be too lazy to tame your own creatures! After all, they're way too needy with all the feeding, temperature requirements, shelter, a bed. Do you think it's a Bed & Breakfast we're running here? Tame it straight out of the box and get to work! You will get randomly assigned sex and genetics.

Find the new "Beasts" tab in the workshop and buy and unlock all the beasts you want

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):

Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
