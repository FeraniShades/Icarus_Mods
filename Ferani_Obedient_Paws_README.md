All tamed pets and mounts can follow orders: Follow, Wander, Stay and Lie Down. Part of the Obedience Training-collection.

Finally some order around here.

All animals can follow all orders - Follow, Wander, Stay and Lie Down. Now you can count them without having to start over all the time!

Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_GrantedAuras
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats
D_Mounts

If you have any other mods that affect the mount movement states, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Known bugs:
Any animals based on the Boar Skeleton (goes for boars, pigs and dribbos) have a bugged animation montage, where they will sit to howl before going to sleep, and clipping through their paws. It's a game bug, not a mod bug, but I'm working on a small animation fix!

If you come across any bugs, don't hesitate to reach out! 

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Extract Mod from PAK File"
Choose the PAK-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP)
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" (double-click on the mod name)
Merge it with other mods / by itself
Play!
