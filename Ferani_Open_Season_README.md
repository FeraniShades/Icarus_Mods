Find the new Open Season creatures in the Workshop and buy as many as you want. Wild creatures on demand in any biome, we won't ask what you need them for - they're yours if you can pay! 
For achievement hunting, bestiary points, and gear / mod tests.

To make gameplay extra spicy and exciting, you can buy bundles of creatures to release all at once for maximum chaos. 

Important notes:
Creatures spawn at Level 50
The creatures will spawn 10 seconds after opening the crate to give you time to get out of the way
The crate will disappear when opened, but you can still see its shadow. It will disappear again after the creature has spawned
The creatures follow the game's normal AI behavior for wild creatures, meaning they despawn when you log out or go too far away from the general area
If you activate the crate but log out before it spawns its creature, the crate will return to its original position when you log back in and be reset to try again
Compatible with other Workshop mods, including Ferani's Buyable Beasts and Bluebacks
These are wild creatures, so they are not tamed beforehand. If you want to order tamed beasts, you can use my other mod, Buyable Beasts. The usual wild creatures requiring the bait and trap are still tamable through normal means

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager (an EXMODZ-file directly in the "\Icarus\Content\Paks\Mods" folder will not work)
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!

Troubleshooting:
Icarus Mod Manager: 
If you are using the mod manager and have trouble with the mod, try to click "Check for IMM Update" and "Update Data Folder" on the right side, and then re-merge your mods. This fixes about 90% of the issues, such as missing or wrong icons and random weird behaviors.

PAK-files:
PAK-files are more likely to stop working after the weekly updates, so use at your own risk. Try using the EXMODZ-version with the Icarus Mod Manager instead. If the PAK-version breaks, send me a message and I will try to update it.
