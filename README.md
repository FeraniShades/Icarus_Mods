# Icarus Mods by Ferani
Mods made for the game Icarus by Ferani Shades.

## Regular mods

### [Ferani's Baby Boom](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/310)

Higher chance of wild animals spawning with a baby.

### [Ferani's Barren Lands](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/222)

Cosmetic foliage is disabled and won't spawn. Only harvestable plants are left.

### [Ferani's Bluebacks](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/169)

Bluebacks and Redbacks are now ridable and tamable! Find them in the wild and tame a new mount, or order one from the Workshop.

### [Ferani's Building Stability](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/210) [OUTDATED]

Building stability increased, huge building range for any building material, both horizontal and vertically. 

- This mod is now outdated, instead use Ferani's Steadfast Structures.

### [Ferani's Buyable Beasts](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/278)

All wild mounts and tames can be purchased in the Workshop.

### [Ferani's Buyable Zebras](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/156) [OUTDATED]

The zebra mounts from the Zebra Rescue Mission DLC are now available for purchase at the workshop! Buy as many striped best friends as you wish.

- This mod is now outdated, instead use Ferani's Buyable Beasts.

### [Ferani's Compatible Ammo](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/161)

Any ammunition is compatible with any firearm. Arrows and bolts can be used by both bows and crossbows. Any ammunition can be used for turrets.

### [Ferani's Effortless Lights](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/173)

Lights and candles don't need power or fuel.

### [Ferani's Heated Animal Bed](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/155)

The Animal Heated Bed now actually is warm. 

### [Ferani's Homestead Expanded](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/223)

Adds a bunch of new functionality to Homestead items! Requires the Homestead DLC.

### [Ferani's Lava-Proofing](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/172)

You can build in lava without your materials burning up. Great for building bridges in volcanic areas.

### [Ferani's Mount Compass](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/168)

Mount icons on the compass have no max distance. It might make your compass a little crowded... But at least you can find your pets!

### [Ferani's Open Season](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/355)

Buy any wild creature from the Workshop for custom hunts, including bundled animals and Great Hunts bosses.

### [Ferani's Steadfast Structures](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/339)

The game mechanic for building stability is disabled, meaning free builds and placements with less lag.

## The Obedience Training Collection

Ferani's Obedience Training is a collection of mods to help you keep your tamed animals in check!

### [Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/215)

Base mod: required for Ferani's Obedience Training-collection.

### [Ferani's Fall-proof Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/231)

Your pets take no fall damage.

### [Ferani's Foraging Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/217)

All animals have the grazing ability, so they can find their own food. 

### [Ferani's Gentle Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/176)

Tamed pets and mounts cause no damage to your buildings and deployables. 

### [Ferani's Hungry Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/179)

Your tames and mounts can eat human food and gain buffs, and have 2 extra stomach spaces. 

### [Ferani's Lava-proof Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/178)

Tamed animals and mounts are fully immune to lava and will not catch fire if they touch it. 

### [Ferani's Obedient Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/171)

All tamed pets and mounts can follow orders: Follow, Wander, Stay and Lie Down. 

### [Ferani's Padded Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/216)

Mounts and tames do not take damage from defensive structures, like spikes and hedgehogs. 

### [Ferani's Sneaky Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/218)

Mounts and tames have significantly less threat, so they won't be spotted as easily. Juveniles do not get attacked at all. 

### [Ferani's Speedy Paws](https://www.nexusmods.com/icarus/mods/177)

Mounts follow you faster while traveling, so they don't fall behind and get lost. Part of the Obedience Training Collection.
