Aww, it's adorable! Can we keep it?

Babies galore! All wild tamable animals have a 50% chance of spawning with a juvenile. Meaning moas have slightly fewer babies, but raptor and slinker babies are now much easier to find.

The most heartbreaking part is having to kill their parent in front of them to start taming them... Shh, don't cry. I am your mother now.

Used files:
D_Tames
If you have any other mods that affect the juvenile spawn chances they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Known issues:
If you kill the parent but the taming option isn't available on the juvenile, it means there's another adult nearby that the baby now considers its parent. Kill the other adults in the area as well, then the baby will be available to tame.

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager (an EXMODZ-file directly in the Icarus folder will not work)
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
