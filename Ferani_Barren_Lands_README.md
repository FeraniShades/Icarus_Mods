If only weeding my garden was this easy...

All cosmetic foliage in the game is disabled and won't spawn in. No grass, mini bushes, stalks, seaweed, nothing! I feel like I can hear an echo now?

Used files:
LGT_AC_Tundra
LGT_AC_Tundra_DLC2
LGT_CF_Grass2
LGT_GL_Grass2
LGT_GL_Needles
LGT_SW_Grass
LGT_SW_Moss1
LGT_SW_Moss2
LGT_WL_Reeds

If you have any other mods that affect foliage spawning in the game, they might conflict so make sure to check.

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP)
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" (double-click on the mod name)
Merge it with other mods / by itself
Play!
