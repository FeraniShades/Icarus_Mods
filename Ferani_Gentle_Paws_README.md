Tamed pets and mounts cause no damage to your buildings and deployables. Part of the Obedience Training Collection.

No, no beasties in the house, no no, my machines!! Ah, there she goes...

Have you ever been minding your business in your base, smelting and crafting in peace, only for your mount to chase a wolf inside the house and trample around on everything? They cause way more damage than the intruder does! 

They're now more gentle, and your animals do no damage to buildings and deployables... They will no longer ruin your hard work when they stomp or thrash around inside the house. As for wild intruding beasts, you might still wanna build a fence.

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats
D_GrantedAuras

If you have any other mods that affect the stats of tamed animals, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):

Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
