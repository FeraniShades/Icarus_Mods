Mounts and tames do not take damage from defensive structures, like spikes and hedgehogs. Part of the Obedience Training-collection.

There's simply no time to babysit your own animals when hordes of beasts come running at your fortification!

Defend as a team, player and pet! Since they're not smart enough to walk around spiky obstacles, we can at least make them immune to them, so we don't lose our own during defensive battles.

No zebras were harmed in the making of this mod........ okay maybe one, but he had it coming.

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats
D_GrantedAuras
BP_Spike_Trap_Base

If you have any other mods that affect the modifier states of tamed animals or the BP_Spike_Trap_Base, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
Feedback and suggestions are welcome!
