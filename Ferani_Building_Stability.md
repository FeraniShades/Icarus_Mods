Building stability increased, huge building range for any building material, both horizontal and vertically.

Completely stable buildings, no therapist needed.

Why, oh, why are my building plans always foiled by something so intrusive as "physics"... No more! 
Buildings are fully stable now, no matter how crazy you build! Every type from thatch and wood to concrete and limestone, all buildings can go on forever. They can even float in the air with no supports... come on, it's a foreign planet, that's not the craziest concept in the world!

The stability colors still show, so you can tell which pieces would normally need support in the vanilla game. However, no matter how red the icon is, it will not break or crack. Perfect for skyscrapers, endless bridges, or stairs all the way to the heavens.

Used files:

D_BuildingStability
If you have any other mods that affect D_BuildingStability, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):

Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
