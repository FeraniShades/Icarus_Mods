Your tames and mounts can eat human food and gain buffs, and have 2 extra stomach spaces. Part of the Obedience Training-collection.

Hmm, human food? I like it.

Your animals can now eat your table scraps and gourmet dishes, whatever you wanna feed them. They will gain all the buffs that come with the food, but will not have stats of abilities they don't have, such as projective damage and crafting speed. They do not gain buffs from liquids though, only fill their thirst-bar.

Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_GrantedAuras
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats

If you have any other mods that affect the modifier states of food, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

Feel free to message me if you come across any bugs that need patching.

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):

Download the EXMODZ-file
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Import EXMODZ File"
Choose the EXMODZ-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP), and it will show up on the list on the left
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" by double-clicking on the mod name
Merge it by itself or with other mods (there may be conflicts, so keep an eye on what is being merged)
Play!
