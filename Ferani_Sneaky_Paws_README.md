No sneak attacks on your delicious pets.

Mounts and tames have significantly less threat, so they won't be spotted as easily. Juveniles do not get attacked at all.
 
Used files:
D_TamedCreatureModifiers
D_GrantedAuras
D_ModifierStates
D_Stats

If you have any other mods that affect the modifier states, they might conflict, so make sure to check.

If you come across any bugs, don't hesitate to reach out! 

Ferani's Obedience Training Base Mod is required for the mod to work!

Installation:

WITH ICARUS MOD MANAGER by JIMK72 (RECOMMENDED):
Open your Mod Manager
On the right side, choose "Extract Mod from PAK File"
Choose the PAK-file you just downloaded (extract it from ZIP)
Add it to your list of "Mods to Install" (double-click on the mod name)
Merge it with other mods / by itself
Play!
